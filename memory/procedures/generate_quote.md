# PROCEDURE: How to Generate a Quote

When a client asks for a pricing estimate or quote, follow these exact steps:

1. **Acknowledge and Validate:** Thank the client for reaching out to T.S Industries and express excitement about their project.
2. **Scope Inquiry:** Ask 3 to 4 highly specific questions about their requirements. For example, ask if they need a database, user authentication, or mobile responsiveness.
3. **Draft the Baseline:** Once scope is provided, list the components required (Frontend, Backend, Database).
4. **Apply Rules:** Check `memory/rules.md` to ensure any requested discounts do not violate the 13.47% floor constraint.
5. **Call to Action:** End the message by asking the client to confirm the scope so a formal PDF quotation can be generated.
