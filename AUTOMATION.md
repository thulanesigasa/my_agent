# Approval-only automation mode

This mode coordinates the existing local services. It prepares eligible work without requiring repeated sync, campaign creation, follow-up preparation or weekly snapshot actions. **Every outgoing email still requires Approve & Send.** No email is sent merely by enabling the mode or leaving the dashboard open.

## One-time setup on your Windows installation

1. Stop both servers, run `git pull --ff-only`, and restart the backend and frontend. No new dependencies or credentials are required. Keep your existing Gmail token and environment files locally.
2. Open **Settings → Automation mode**. The configuration starts paused for each connected mailbox. Review Gmail connection/readiness below if it has not already been verified.
3. Enter a target town and business type. New initial drafts match the saved town exactly after case/whitespace normalization, and the business type must occur in the saved industry text. Empty labels include all eligible saved leads. These labels are not geocoding or proof of a source's location. Replies and follow-ups remain scoped to tracked business conversations in the connected mailbox.
4. Start with three automatic business draft attempts per SAST day, choose a starting time, and decide whether new research/initial outreach/follow-ups should run on weekdays only. Supported reply drafting and Gmail polling continue every day.
5. Optionally enable daily Tavily research. This also requires your existing local key, `ALLOW_TAVILY_RESEARCH=true` and the research worker. Choose 1–5 results and 1–5 maximum requests per job. It can consume provider credits; the shared daily/monthly limits, cooldowns and caches still apply. Leave it disabled to avoid new scheduled research requests. No paid model is required for template drafts.
6. Leave separately activated campaign and follow-up schedules paused when using this mode, so their independent quotas do not add work. Existing standalone controls are retained for users who choose them.
7. Click **Save and enable automation**, keep the backend running, and use **Approvals** for outgoing messages. That page refreshes every 15 seconds while visible and when you are not editing or performing an action. It does not approve anything automatically.

`AUTOMATION_ENABLED=true` enables the local worker by default, but a mailbox must still be explicitly enabled in Settings. Set the environment option to false and restart to disable the worker. The mode does not require `ENABLE_SCHEDULER`, an extra worker process or a paid host. A stopped/sleeping PC cannot run local jobs. Do not enable multiple independent Gmail pollers unnecessarily; the shared sync lease prevents overlapping syncs.

## Automatic work

- **Gmail polling:** at most one coordinator tick every two minutes, persisted across restart and guarded by a process lease. Sync downloads tracked conversations and applies existing opt-out suppression. Gmail failures are recorded, retried on later ticks, and prevent reply/follow-up drafting in that tick. No resend is attempted.
- **Reply handling:** local rules assess the latest matched human business reply. Clear interest/questions can produce the existing scope-discussion template in Approvals. Tests, unrelated mail, automated notices, bounces and opt-outs are excluded. Ambiguous messages stay unclassified/needs review; no price, contract, completed project or sale is invented. Existing reply keys preserve edited, rejected, failed and sent approvals.
- **Research:** after the configured SAST start time on allowed days, one deterministic research job is created per mailbox/day. The existing worker discovers, inspects and assesses within its total request budget. Repeated ticks, changed settings and lost responses cannot create another coordinator research job that day. Another active research job can delay creation. Missing configuration/budget/provider failures appear in history. Paused or interrupted jobs are not silently resumed.
- **Initial proposals:** matching saved leads passing all current identity, activity, website, contact and permission gates get portfolio proposals automatically. Existing approvals and historical sends are respected. New research jobs can automatically qualify supported structured source records under [QUALIFICATION.md](QUALIFICATION.md), with explicit automated provenance. They are never stamped as human-verified or granted recipient consent.
- **Follow-ups:** automatically consider tracked initial outreach after seven elapsed days, successful fresh sync including the original thread, current qualification/permission and every existing no-response guard. They do not need a separately enabled follow-up schedule. Exactly one follow-up approval per conversation remains the limit.
- **Weekly reports:** automatically save the most recent completed SAST week's immutable snapshot. By default, queue its email to the connected mailbox with the existing Excel attachment. The report key and approval key are stable, so refreshes do not duplicate it or rewrite a saved/rejected approval. Report emails still require approval. Turning off report emails keeps automatic snapshots enabled.

The mode shares one atomic per-mailbox/SAST-day budget of 1–10 business draft attempts (default 3) between its reply, follow-up and initial drafting. Replies have priority, then follow-ups, then initial proposals. The new-outreach time/weekday applies to the latter two and research; supported replies can be prepared outside that window. Failed/skipped admitted attempts consume a slot, and a target is attempted at most once per day. Edits, pause/resume, retries and restart do not reset usage. Report drafts do not consume this business budget. Separate campaign/follow-up/manual controls and their limits remain independent. Sending is still governed by its separate persistent pause/daily attempt controls.

## What is deliberately not fabricated

Source extraction cannot prove identity/current activity, website absence or recipient permission merely because an email address was found. The supported two-source qualifier can populate factual evidence with automated provenance; candidates without the required source evidence are skipped automatically and appear in the mode's history (up to 20 matching-lead exceptions per tick). **Automatic qualification supports only the strict structured evidence described in QUALIFICATION.md; it cannot qualify arbitrary pages.** If no candidates are already qualified and permitted, automatic research may produce a shortlist without producing outreach approvals. Those candidates require real missing evidence before they can enter outreach; the app does not bypass the qualification/consent rules to satisfy a draft target.

Automatic report suggestions now count local-rule categories for the latest human reply in each cohort conversation. They are displayed separately from operator-confirmed interested/declined/won outcomes and exported with distinct Excel labels. Acknowledgments are not interest, and automatic suggestions never mark a sale Won. Saved snapshots retain the rules/results at their original snapshot time. The reports do not require an operator to classify messages just to show the automatic breakdown; confirmed sales outcomes still require evidence.

## Pause, failure and exceptions

The Settings card and Overview show saved mode state, worker configuration, usage and last tick start. Expand **Recent work and skipped candidates** for stage results, approval/research IDs, provider blocks and qualification exceptions. The last-started timestamp is not proof that a tick succeeded or a worker is currently alive.

Saving paused stops new coordinator stages and requests a pause on its own research jobs. An already admitted local/provider step may finish; saved approvals remain available. It does not disable independently activated legacy schedules, cancel someone else's research job, revoke approvals or pause business sending. Use the existing Sending controls when you want to block business dispatch.

An unexpected failure pauses the mode and records partial progress. A running record recovered after a process interruption also pauses its mailbox instead of replaying a possibly completed step. Review existing Approvals and saved progress before re-enabling. Stable approval/job IDs and durable daily reservations protect the crash-after-write boundary. Gmail sync failures and known per-candidate eligibility errors are recorded as blocked/skipped work; they do not fabricate success. If a provider submission from a manually approved send is uncertain, it stays held for evidence-based reconciliation; the coordinator never resends it automatically.

Private operator API: `GET /api/automation` and version-checked `PATCH /api/automation`. Both use the existing API-key protection and current-mailbox scoping. No auto-send switch or public trigger was added.

Implementation is covered by offline fake-provider tests. No actual business outreach, provider research or Windows UI pilot was run during development. This code does not make previous incomplete business verification or live-pilot validation complete.
